# SIGNAL — three skills that kill the noise in knowledge work

**One for what you send. One for what you receive. One for when it's already a mess.**

AI made everyone louder; nobody got clearer. Long emails, long docs, 15-minute Looms — generated in seconds, skimmed in less. If your work moves through other people — client and agency, manager and team, consultant and stakeholder — the noise is your whole day. SIGNAL stands at the three points where it wins.

**Demos + full story:** [the SIGNAL page](https://demetrious.co/skills/signal)

---

## `dco-great-feedback` — when you send

> Forces vague, subjective feedback to become clear, evidenced, and actionable — before you send it.

"Make it pop." "Something's off." Your reaction is real, but without the what/why/direction, whoever made it is guessing — and guessing costs revisions. This skill interrogates you (one question at a time, researching reference examples *for* you), logs evidence, runs a stickler's readiness gate, and packages a handoff folder built for the receiver's AI: `FEEDBACK.md` + `INDEX.md` + assets. Overridden gate items ship labeled *hunch*, not fact.

**Say:** "help me give feedback" · "I don't love this but can't explain why" · "make my notes clear"

## `dco-bluf-briefer` — when you receive

> Reads the 15,000 words so you get 300.

A 15-minute Loom, a long email, a folder of screenshots — the ask buried at minute 11. The briefer acquires everything, digests it into fixed buckets (decisions / problems / buried asks / deadlines / open questions / FYI), gives every item ONE verdict — act now, decide, can wait, FYI — and hands you `SITREP.md`: ~350 words, one screen, bottom line first, every line pointing back to its source like `[loom ~11:20]`.

**Say:** "brief me on this" · "do I need to worry about anything in here?" · "I don't have time to watch this"

## `dco-bluf-sitrep` — when it's already a mess

> Rebuilds the whole story into a log of truth — and hands you a plan.

Weeks of threads, multiple people, contradicting claims. The sitrep reconstructs a chronological ledger where claims are never recorded as facts ("Sam CLAIMED [L-04]; Riley DISPUTED [L-05]"), holds contradictions in tension instead of guessing, surfaces open loops with ages — including what YOU owe — and delivers `SITREP-01.md` (state of play + prioritized plan, loops grouped by person) plus `LOG.md` (the full story, readable start to finish). Run it again next week: it reports the delta.

**Say:** "I need a sitrep on this project" · "where are we on this?" · "who's waiting on what?"

## The ecosystem move

The skills know each other. A package sent with `dco-great-feedback` is recognized natively by the receiver's `dco-bluf-briefer`. Your AI writes it; their AI reads it. AI to AI — with both humans finally on the same page.
