# The philosophy: less noise, more signal

**Bottom line: AI made everyone louder. These skills exist to make people clearer.**

## The problem

Knowledge work is communication. And AI broke its economics: producing words is now free, but *reading* them still costs what it always cost. So we generate long emails, long documents, long Looms — and skim each other's. Nobody gives their AI real context, so it adds noise instead of cutting it. We look up from our screens and we've lost the plot of our own projects.

The breakdown concentrates wherever work moves through other people:

- **Client ↔ agency** — subjective work discussed by people who don't share taste or vocabulary
- **Manager ↔ team** — asks buried in minute 11 of a video update
- **Consultant ↔ stakeholder** — three threads, two versions of the truth
- **Anyone ↔ everyone** — the reply you owe but can't articulate

## BLUF: the borrowed doctrine

**Bottom Line Up Front** is how military briefings work: the verdict first, the detail after — because the reader might only get ten seconds. It's the single highest-leverage writing habit in knowledge work, and it's the spine of this library:

- The **briefer** briefs you like you're the general: verdict, then buckets in triage order, every line pointing to its source.
- The **sitrep** opens with the state of play, never the history.
- **great-feedback** puts the TL;DR verdict at the top of every package.

## The three points

Noise wins at exactly three moments, so the SIGNAL suite has exactly three skills:

1. **When you send** — your subjective reaction leaves as a recipe, not a vibe (`dco-great-feedback`)
2. **When you receive** — any pile becomes one screen, verdict first (`dco-bluf-briefer`)
3. **When it's already a mess** — the whole story rebuilt into a log of truth and a plan (`dco-bluf-sitrep`)

Sender-side. Receiver-side. Recovery. Three points, one pipe.

## Design principles (all suites, forever)

- **Verdicts, not hedges.** A skill commits to one triage per item. Wrong-but-correctable beats vague-but-safe.
- **Claims are not facts.** Reported state and verified state stay separate, always ("Sam CLAIMED; Riley DISPUTED").
- **Sources or it didn't happen.** Every extracted item points back to where it came from.
- **The human stays the expert.** Skills inform judgment; they never prescribe the maker's process.
- **Inspectable, free, no gates.** Plain markdown you can read, from a person, not a platform.
