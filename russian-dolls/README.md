# RUSSIAN DOLLS — four skills that turn a scoped project into the hands of the people doing it

**Broad to specific. One level per doll. From Anya to Zoya — A to Z.**

The client said yes. Now what? The gap right after "yes" is where projects quietly rot: nobody has the actual *shape* of the work, so nobody can sequence it, so nobody knows what to hand to whom. Russian Dolls descends that gap in four honest levels — each doll does one altitude and refuses the next one down.

## `dco-anya-shape` — level 1, the shape

> Turns a scoped project into a clean map of workstreams.

Anya takes what you already agreed to — a brief, a proposal, a kickoff transcript, an email thread — interrogates you about what's actually in scope, and draws **the shape**: the workstreams. Not steps, not tools, not people. Output: `01-SHAPE.md`.

**Say:** "break down this project" · "what's the shape of this" · "we just closed this client, now what" · "start the dolls"

## `dco-olga-breakdown` — level 2, the breakdown

> Zooms every workstream into sequenced chunks with clear outcomes.

Olga reads Anya's shape and cuts each workstream into **chunks**, each with a stated "done means." Order matters to her; nothing floats. Output: `02-BREAKDOWN.md`.

**Say:** "open the next doll" · "what are the phases" · "chunk this out" · "run Olga"

## `dco-vera-steps` — level 3, the steps

> Turns chunks into executable steps, grouped into sprints.

Vera makes it startable: every chunk becomes actions someone can actually begin, grouped into sprints, with hints for whatever tools *you* named — she asks, she doesn't assume. Output: `03-STEPS.md`.

**Say:** "make this executable" · "give me the steps" · "what do I actually do" · "run Vera"

## `dco-zoya-packets` — level 4, the packets

> Splits the project into a standalone packet per person, with overlap contracts.

The smallest doll, and the one the whole chain was for. Every collaborator gets only their part — plus an **overlap contract**: what they need from others, and what others need from them. Works solo too (packets by work-mode instead of by person). Output: `packets/PACKET-<person>.md`.

**Say:** "who does what" · "split this by person" · "what do I send my developer" · "last doll"

## How the chain works

Each doll looks for the previous doll's output file and picks up from there. Miss a level and the doll tells you which one to run first instead of guessing. Every output is markdown with a note to your AI, so any future session can continue the chain — and when reality changes the plan, you re-open the doll at the level that changed, not the whole thing.

**The boundary:** Russian Dolls is post-agreement. It answers *how*, never *whether* — pricing, proposals, and should-we-do-this are a different job, and the dolls will say so.
